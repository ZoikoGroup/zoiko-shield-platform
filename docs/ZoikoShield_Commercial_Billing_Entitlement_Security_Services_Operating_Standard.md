# 5. A. Commercial Account, Organization, Legal Entity & Tenant Model

**A1. What is the top-level customer object?**

**Decision:** A commercial customer account contracts for one or more tenant environments. The commercial account, tenant, legal entity, business unit and environment are separate objects.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Enterprise buyers may purchase centrally while security data, response authority and evidence remain scoped to different subsidiaries/regions.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Persist group_account/commercial_account, tenant_id, legal_entity_id, business_unit_id, environment_id and region independently. Never infer commercial payer or response authority from tenant name alone.

**A2. Can one customer operate multiple legal entities and regions?**

**Decision:** Yes, where the contracted deployment class and market registry permit it.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Financial services and enterprise SaaS buyers often need regional/legal separation without fragmenting the enterprise relationship.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Each tenant/environment binds to allowed regions, data/retention policy, legal entity and service scope. Cross-entity reporting is a governed aggregation, not data-plane collapse.

**A3. Who is allowed to change the ZoikoShield subscription or commercial payment method?**

**Decision:** Commercial Account Owner and Billing Admin, subject to enterprise procurement and dual-control policies.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Security analysts should not inherit commercial authority simply because they administer the platform.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Commercial roles are separate from SOC, responder, auditor and security-admin roles. Step-up MFA and audit are required for payment method, renewal, legal entity and material contract changes.

**A4. Can MSP/MSSP users operate multiple customer tenants?**

**Decision:** Yes only through explicit, non-transitive delegated access.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Partner scale must not weaken tenant isolation or create accidental reseller entitlement.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Partner identities use managing-organization context, customer-visible grants, expiry/revocation and per-customer role scope. Commercial discounts are not inferred from access.

**A5. What happens when a customer subsidiary or business unit is sold or separated?**

**Decision:** Commercial and tenant separation follows an approved transfer/migration plan; evidence lineage is preserved.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Cybersecurity records and contractual obligations may need to follow different legal entities after a corporate transaction.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Create transfer records, effective boundaries, data/export/legal-hold decisions, entitlement mapping and post-transfer reconciliation. Never reassign historical evidence by bulk edit.

# 6. B. Offer Catalog, Design Partners, Pilots, Entitlements & Upgrades

**B1. What are the canonical launch offers?**

**Decision:** Managed Defense and Continuous Assurance are the two independent entry offers. Additional modules/services are phase-gated.

**Commercial / product rationale:** This matches the controlled market architecture and supports both security-led and assurance-led buyers.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Stable internal product/SKU IDs remain independent of display names. Bundle rules express INCLUDED_BY, REQUIRES, ALTERNATIVE_TO, INCOMPATIBLE_WITH and OVERRIDES relationships.

**B2. Are public prices locked?**

**Decision:** No. The approved architecture keeps ADR-06 open and defaults to bespoke design-partner pricing with no public price until Finance/Commercial approval.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Publishing invented prices would create commercial drift and force engineering to encode an unapproved business model.

**Engineering / operating rule:** The checkout/CPQ path refuses any price mapping lacking APPROVED status, region/currency scope and effective date.

**B3. Does ZoikoShield have a free trial?**

**Decision:** No unrestricted free-trial claim is assumed. Design partner, evaluation and pilot access are explicit programs.

**Commercial / product rationale:** A security platform may receive privileged telemetry, customer credentials and human service; uncontrolled trial access is operationally and legally unsafe.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Use evaluation_program_id/pilot_id with duration, data classes, connectors, entitlements, service coverage, allowed response authority, region, payment requirement, conversion policy and expiry action.

**B4. How do upgrades work?**

**Decision:** Upgrade only after a commercial preview/order change is approved and required deployment/service capacity is ready.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Granting high-cost retention, region, dedicated environment or managed service before capacity is confirmed creates negative margin and false claims.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Use pending entitlement activation with claim_eligibility. Runtime allows new scope only after commercial + technical + operational prerequisites pass.

**B5. How do downgrades work?**

**Decision:** At renewal or agreed effective date by default, subject to evidence-retention and active-incident safety checks.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Immediate removal of retention, connectors or response capability can damage an investigation or breach evidence obligations.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Schedule downgrade; detect incompatible configuration, active incidents, legal holds and retention obligations; require remediation rather than deleting history.

**B6. Can Sales grant free modules indefinitely?**

**Decision:** No.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Unbounded concessions conceal cost and create renewal disputes.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Every concession has scope, start/end, commercial reason, approver, margin impact and renewal treatment. Expired exceptions fail closed.

**C1. Can discovery automatically increase the bill?**

**Decision:** No. Discovered assets are candidate scope until the meter/contract policy accepts them.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Attack-surface discovery should improve security, not surprise the customer with charges.

**Engineering / operating rule:** New resources enter DISCOVERED → REVIEW_REQUIRED → COVERED/BILLABLE or EXCLUDED. Auto-enrollment is permitted only under an explicit threshold/authorization policy with notice and cap.

**C2. Can one physical resource count under multiple commercial metrics?**

**Decision:** Only when the catalog intentionally sells distinct services and clearly discloses the overlap.

**Commercial / product rationale:** MDR, exposure and identity coverage can observe the same environment; accidental double counting is unacceptable.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Bundle validator and billing preview show overlap. A duplicate resource across sources must resolve to one canonical resource ID per metric family.

**C3. How are seasonal or ephemeral resources treated?**

**Decision:** Through versioned aggregation/minimum-duration rules, not manual guesswork.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Cloud workloads can churn rapidly and make naive “resource count” pricing unstable.

**Decision:** An approved meter_definition + source scope + unit + aggregation window + contract authorization + visible customer policy.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Rejected/quarantined records and platform-side processing loss do not count toward consumption; committed minimums are handled by contract, not by fabricating usage.

**Decision:** Through immutable usage evidence, reconciliation and a controlled correction record.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Editable aggregate totals are not defensible.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Raw accepted meter events remain append-preserving; corrections use reversal/replacement/adjustment events and regenerate the billing export under approval.

**D4. How is overage prevented from becoming a surprise?**

**Decision:** Forecast and thresholds before overage, with approved caps/authorization behavior.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Telemetry can spike during attacks — exactly when surprise cost is least acceptable.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Admin sees committed quantity, current usage, forecast, warning thresholds, overage rate/policy and action path. Contract may cap, throttle non-critical ingestion, move to protected overage, or require approval; it must not silently drop critical evidence.

**R1. Can a support agent extend a customer entitlement to fix an incident?**

**Decision:** Only through a controlled, time-bounded emergency entitlement with approval and audit.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Operational pressure must not create undocumented permanent commercial rights.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Emergency entitlement stores reason, scope, approver, expiry, incident/case reference and renewal/billing treatment. It cannot elevate response authority beyond customer authorization.

**R2. Can a discount or late payment reduce evidence integrity features?**

**Decision:** No for the integrity controls required to keep previously generated records trustworthy.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Evidence integrity is a platform safety invariant, not a premium cosmetic feature.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Commercial entitlements may control retention duration, package/export capability or advanced verifier features prospectively, but stored evidence must remain internally integrity-protected and historically reproducible.

**30.1. What is the commercial kill switch?**

**Decision:** A controlled ability to stop new quotes/orders, entitlement expansion, usage export, invoice finalization or selected charging while preserving customer security operations and evidence.

**Commercial / product rationale:** In a commercial defect, stop financial harm without disabling defense.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Granular by region/catalog/meter/customer/channel. Requires privileged approval, audit, reconciliation and restart procedure.

**30.2. What is the security-service kill switch?**

**Decision:** A separate safety mechanism to freeze selected automated response/action paths or provider integrations while retaining observability and evidence.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Commercial and security incidents require different containment levers.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Action freeze cannot be triggered by payment state. Customer/Zoiko emergency procedures follow response authority and incident governance.

**30.3. How are customer-impacting incidents communicated?**

**Decision:** Through approved operational/status communication based on confirmed impact, with separate commercial remedy workflow.

**Commercial / product rationale:** Do not mix unverified monitoring signals with refunds or sales promises.

**Engineering / operating rule:** Incident communications identify affected service/region, time, workaround and next update; SLA/service-credit review follows evidence after the fact.

# 33. Final CTO / Commercial Doctrine

<table>

<th><p><strong>ONE PLATFORM — TWO ENTRY OFFERS — TWO PLANES — ZERO COMMERCIAL AMBIGUITY</strong></p>
<p>ZoikoShield exists to make cybersecurity defensible. Its commercial system must therefore be as evidence-led as its security system. Every charge has an approved basis. Every protected resource has a controlled definition. Every billable byte or unit is reconciled. Every human service has an obligation and delivery record. Every claim has evidence and expiry. Every security event remains a security fact before it is ever considered a commercial input. When scope, price, quantity, region, service capacity, claim status or authority is uncertain, the system must refuse to guess.</p></th>

> **•** Commercial truth is versioned and governed; code, website copy and sales intuition do not invent price or scope.
>
> **•** Security truth is append-preserving; incidents, detections, controls and evidence are never rewritten to fit billing outcomes.
>
> **•** Managed Defense and Continuous Assurance are independently purchasable, independently operable and safely combinable.
>
> **•** The customer is not charged more simply because attacks, alerts, vulnerabilities, failed controls or AI complexity increase.
>
> **•** Protected-resource and telemetry meters are explicit, deduplicated, customer-visible, forecastable and correction-safe.
>
> **•** Human service obligations are first-class commercial objects; software entitlement never implies 24/7 staffing, response authority or advisory time.
>
> **•** Commercial purchase never grants high-impact response authority; security actions remain governed by customer authorization and safety controls.
>
> **•** Evidence integrity is a platform invariant. Retention and advanced packaging may be commercial dimensions, but prior proof cannot be erased to enforce monetization.
>
> **•** Zoiko One is a distribution and integration advantage, not a prerequisite and not a second charging path for the same scope.
>
> **•** Sector packs extend one horizontal core through controlled content, mappings, integrations, reports and contract terms — never through product forks or unsupported compliance promises.
>
> **•** AI is governed as a subsystem and cost surface. Provider/model volatility is not an excuse for opaque customer charges or reduced human accountability.
>
> **•** Global design means controlled regional availability, residency and obligations — not universal claims.
>
> **•** Production readiness is evidenced through unit economics, reconciliation, negative testing, service capacity, security assurance, claim control, recovery and signed acceptance — not by the existence of a pricing page.
